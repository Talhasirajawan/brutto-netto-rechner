01 · The friction limit

Wheel brakes can only stop as fast as the tires grip

Kinetic energy grows with the square of speed (E = ½mv²), but deceleration is bounded by roughly μ·g. On fast, low-friction roads that limit gets brutal.

02 · Why thrusters

A braking force that doesn't depend on the tires

Oct 2026 — a Tesla Roadster flew on SpaceX cold-gas thrusters: ~80 kN from ~250 kg of compressed air. Forward-facing nozzles could, in principle, decelerate independent of road friction.

04 · The control problem

A thruster is not a drop-in brake

Off-axis thrust creates pitch & yaw moments, propellant is finite, and ~4 g acts on the occupants. Hand-tuned rules won't stay safe across every speed and surface — so learn it, under hard constraints.

06 · Research questions & hypotheses

What the study will answer

RQ1 · Benefit

How much better?

Stopping-distance reduction & collision-avoidance gain vs. friction-only AEB, as a function of speed, friction and obstacle distance.

RQ2 · Safety

Can it stay safe?

Can a constrained RL policy keep yaw rate, sideslip, pitch and occupant load in bounds — even with asymmetric thrust and delayed actuation?

RQ3 · Robustness

What dominates?

Sensitivity to thrust, latency, exhaust velocity, propellant and nozzle geometry — which parameter matters most?

H1 — Above 200 km/h and on low-friction surfaces, thruster-assisted braking avoids a significantly larger fraction of collisions than friction-only AEB.
H2 — A constrained RL controller attains a higher collision-avoidance rate than a fixed-rule thruster trigger at equal constraint-violation rates.
07 · Methodology

Three phases, baseline → thruster → evaluation

PHASE 1

Model & baseline

  • Vehicle digital twin (non-linear tires, load transfer).
  • Friction-only AEB per UN R152.
  • Deep-learning collision predictor with uncertainty.
PHASE 2

Thruster & RL controller

  • Parametric cold-gas thruster model (latency, mass-flow decay, moments).
  • Constrained MDP formulation.
  • Train CPO / Lagrangian PPO–SAC with curriculum + domain randomisation.
PHASE 3

Comparative evaluation

  • Monte Carlo: AEB vs. rule-based vs. learned.
  • Metrics: avoidance rate, stopping distance, impact speed, constraint violations, propellant.
  • Ablations & sensitivity analysis.
